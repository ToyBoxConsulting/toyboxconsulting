The Year-End Operations Tune-Up

Three working sessions in December to find the gaps costing your organization the most, fix them with your team, and start January with a plan.

Year-end is when an organization finds out which systems are real and which ones live in one person's head. The report nobody can find. The filing date nobody wrote down. The process that stops when one person takes a week off.

The Year-End Operations Tune-Up is a fixed-scope ToyBox engagement built for that moment: three working sessions in December, and a January plan your team can run on its own.

Who it's for

  • Nonprofits. Executive directors and board chairs who want the filing dates, the board calendar and the month-end close written down before the new year starts.
  • Small businesses. Owners who have outgrown "we'll figure it out" and want the business to run on a plan instead of on memory.

The three sessions

Session 1: Find it (90 minutes)

The leader and the board chair take ToyBox's free Operational Health Check and Board Governance Self-Assessment. ToyBox reviews the results alongside your budget vs. actual, policies, org chart and filing dates.

You get: a one-page scorecard across five areas (governance and leadership, finance and compliance, people and culture, programs and impact, technology and operations) and your top three risks.

Session 2: Fix it (2 hours, working session)

ToyBox and your team build the three quickest fixes together. For most nonprofits, that means:

  • A 2027 compliance and filing calendar: Form 990, W-9s and 1099s, renewals, grant reports
  • The board's annual calendar: budget approval, the executive director's review, conflict-of-interest forms
  • A month-end close checklist

You get: working versions of each fix, built with the people who will use them.

Session 3: Hand it off (90 minutes)

You get: a 90-day January plan with an owner and a date on every item, every template handed over, and one check-in call in January.

The small business version

Same three sessions, built on the five systems every small business needs:

  • Books closed every month
  • A cash forecast
  • Written roles
  • A contract on every job
  • One home for customer information

Session 1 checks where the business stands on all five. Session 2 builds the fixes for the three that need it most. Session 3 hands over the plan.

What you walk away with

  • A one-page scorecard and your top three risks
  • Three fixes, built and in use
  • A 90-day plan with an owner and a date on every item
  • The templates, yours to keep and reuse next year
  • A check-in call in January

Timing
