Before you start

This playbook is an account of what I did on my own sites. It is not professional advice, and it is not a guarantee that any of it will work the same way on yours. Use it at your own discretion, and take the proper precautions.

That means: back up anything you can't afford to lose before you change it. Don't practice on a live site people depend on. Read what an AI co-pilot writes before you ship it — especially anything touching security headers, privacy language, consent, or analytics. Third-party tools and interfaces change constantly, so check the current documentation rather than trusting a screenshot from September 2026. And if your site is business-critical, handles payments, or sits under a compliance obligation, get someone qualified involved.
