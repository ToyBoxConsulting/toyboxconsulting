How it works

Three sessions, one 90-day plan.

  1. Session 1 · 90 minutes

    Find it

    ToyBox reviews your results and records, then names your top three risks.

  2. Session 2 · 2 hours

    Fix it

    ToyBox and your team build the three quickest fixes together.

  3. Session 3 · 90 minutes

    Hand it off

    You get the 90-day plan, every template and one check-in call 30 days later.

Read more: price, timing and what gets fixed

The Tune-Up is one fixed price, set before any work starts, with a lower rate for smaller organizations. No hourly billing. ToyBox shares the price on the call.

Slots go in the order calls are booked. Sign a ToyBox retainer or Starter Package after your Tune-Up, and the full Tune-Up fee counts toward it.

Typical nonprofit fixes: a filing calendar, the board's annual calendar and a month-end close checklist. Read the Tune-Up guide.
