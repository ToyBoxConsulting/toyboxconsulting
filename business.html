Why this is one firm

Golden Hour Unboxed was both things at once.

It was a fixed-price municipal contract for the City of Bellevue's Cross-Cultural Center Without Walls — with a compliant insurance and risk package, vendor agreements, and a reporting obligation at the end of it.

It was also an evening at the Bellevue Botanical Garden with twenty vendors, catering, and proclamations from both the Bellevue City Council and the King County Executive.

The events work because they are run like public contracts. That is the whole proposition.
